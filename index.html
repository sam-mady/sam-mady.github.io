I'm a Senior Robotics and AI Research Scientist on the Dex Team at Nvidia. I work on whole-body dexterity by scaling reinforcement learning from simulation and sim2real.

Before that, I was on the core team behind Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab. I did my MSc at ETH Zürich, with research at Mila and the Max Planck Institute.
